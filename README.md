## 📌 Project Overview

**LookMovie** is a data analysis project built to transform movie data into an interactive **Power BI dashboard**.

The project demonstrates an end-to-end analytical workflow: data preparation, cleaning and transformation with **Power Query**, analytical modeling, **DAX** calculations, visualization, and insight generation.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective is to analyze the movie catalog and understand **content volume, display quality, genre distribution, ratings, and release growth** across 2025 and 2026.

## ❓ Business Questions

- How large is the movie catalog?
- What percentage of movies are available in FHD quality?
- Which genres have the largest number of titles?
- How are movie releases changing between 2025 and 2026?
- Which genres have the highest and lowest average ratings?
- How is the catalog distributed by display quality?

## 💡 Key Insights

### 1. Strong growth in movie releases

The number of movies increased from **29 in 2025 to 68 in 2026**, representing a **134% growth** in the catalog.

### 2. FHD is the dominant display quality

**86 out of 97 movies (88.7%)** are available in FHD quality, indicating that high-definition content represents the dominant format in the catalog.

### 3. Action leads the catalog by volume

**Action (18 titles)** is the largest genre, followed by **Adventure (17)** and **Horror (14)**. These three genres account for a substantial share of the available titles.

### 4. Drama and Science Fiction show strong ratings

In 2026, **Drama** reaches an average rating of **7.8**, while **Science Fiction** reaches **7.2**, placing both among the strongest-rated genres shown in the dashboard.

### 5. Family has the lowest 2026 average rating

The **Family** genre records a **3.2 average rating in 2026**, considerably below the stronger-performing genres and potentially indicating an area for further content-quality investigation.

## 🎯 Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard results:

- Monitor the rapid increase in catalog volume to ensure content growth remains aligned with user demand.
- Maintain strong FHD availability as it represents the dominant quality format.
- Analyze high-volume genres such as **Action, Adventure, and Horror** to understand their contribution to catalog strategy.
- Investigate the low rating of the **Family** category before expanding content in this segment.
- Continue tracking release growth and ratings over additional years to identify sustainable content trends.
